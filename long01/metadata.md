# Long 01 — Fifth floor in Ancient Rome

**Title:** You Rent the Cheapest Room in Ancient Rome. It's on the Fifth Floor.

**Description:**

In Rome, the higher you live, the less you pay. Not because the view is worse. Because when the building burns, you are the last one out.

Rome, around 100 AD. A million people, most of them living in insulae: tall apartment blocks with shops at street level, rich tenants on the first floor and wooden boxes under the roof. You have just rented one of those boxes.
