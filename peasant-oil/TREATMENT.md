# Tonight You Sleep With the Cow — Impasto Oil Painting version

Style: Impasto Oil Painting (`impasto`). Channel: Ordinary Lives. YouTube Short 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~62 s.
Voice: the channel's ElevenLabs take (same script as the woodcut version), DOM serif subtitles in a calm plane.

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **One room, the light moves (locked frame).** A single painted interior; the fire's light crosses it as the night goes. Elegant, but too static for a Short and for the curfew/November beats.
2. **Grey world, colour re-laid by warmth.** The winter night is painted grey; every warm thing (the cow, the fire, the ember) re-lays the knife strokes around it in colour. At curfew the colour is scraped back to one ember; at the end the light goes out.
3. **A painting in a room.** The film is a genre painting on a wall that we enter and leave. Clever framing, but it delays the hook.

**Chosen: 2.** Warmth is literally the subject, and the medium's native move (grey ↔ colour stroke by stroke) shows it. Opening: macro on a ridge of paint that pulls back to become snow on a thatched roof. Ending: "the light goes out" (raking light sinks until only the ridges glow, then the ember, then black).

## 2. Logline and arc
In a medieval longhouse the warmest thing in your bedroom is a cow, and the night's one job is keeping an ember alive.
Setup: one room for people and animals. Turn: the fire must be covered (curfew) and the herd must shrink (November). Ending: you sleep beside the breathing cow; one ember survives the dark.

## 3. Benchmark
Dutch/Flemish peasant genre interiors (firelit, low ceilings) for value structure; palette-knife night paintings for warm-on-cool knife mosaics. Learn: value plan, warm light vs cool shadow, few decisive strokes. Don't take: compositions, figures.

## 5. Cue map
72 bpm. Solo low strings / hurdy-gurdy-like drone from shot 2; pizzicato under 3–4; near-silence before the bell; bell on "Couvre-feu"; whip-pan whooshes + a soft drum per plate in 7; drone fades to digital zero on the last 0.6 s.

## 6. Sound design
Ambience: winter wind (outside louder in 1 and 6). Foley: palette-knife scrape on every paint-over/repaint, cow breath (1–3, 8–9), fire crackle (2, 4–5), shovel and ash (5), bell (6), whooshes (7), straw (8). Music ducks ~8 dB under the voice. −14 LUFS.

## 7. Type
Subtitles: crisp serif (Cormorant Garamond, OFL) on a calm dark plane, ≤ 2 lines, held ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Titles (COUVRE-FEU / CURFEW / BLOOD MONTH) laid in with the knife.
