**Title:** Your First Day as a Medieval Apprentice (Paris, 1303)

**Description:**

Paris, 1303. You are twelve years old, and this morning your father hands you to an embroiderer — for eight years. No wage, no clock, and no candle: the embroiderers of Paris were forbidden to work after dark, because work done by candlelight came out worse.

This is what an apprenticeship really looked like in medieval Paris: the contract signed before witnesses, the master examined by the guild's sworn men, the family that pays instead of the boy who earns, the inspections, and the long road from apprentice to journeyman to master.
