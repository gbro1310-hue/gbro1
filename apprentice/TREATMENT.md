# Eight Years of Daylight — Ordinary Lives (long-form, ~6.5 min, 16:9)

**Style:** hd-2d pixel diorama (Lemo-Opuscar), same world as the Paris bathhouse Short: pixel billboards in a lit 3D street and a cut-away workshop, tilt-shift + DOF + bloom, RPG interface (quest box, panels, checklists, class change).

**Premise:** Paris, 1303. You are twelve, and your father hands you to an embroiderer for eight years. Second person, channel voice (ElevenLabs), 162 wpm.

**Chapters**
0. Prologue — the walk, the frame, gold thread; title.
1. The Contract — the Book of Trades (Boileau, c. 1268), jurés, the master is examined, one outside apprentice, witnesses, the family pays, 8-year minimum (embroiderers' statutes, 1303).
2. The Light — work runs on daylight; no work by candlelight (embroiderers, 1303); night work banned in many trades; year-one chores.
3. The Week — Sundays and feast days; Saturday ends at the first bell of vespers.
4. The Knock — jurés inspect, seize, fine.
5. Eight Years — skills, class change to journeyman (paid by the day), what a mastership needs (skill, money, oath; in some trades buying the right).
Ending — the window goes grey; the needle goes down; nobody lights a candle.

**Sources (secondary, via search):** F. B. Millett, *Craft-guilds of the thirteenth century in Paris* (1915); C. Bourlet, *Médiévales* 69 (2015); embroiderers' statutes of 1303 (Guillaume de Hangest) as summarised by the Textile Research Centre, Leiden; medievalists.net on guild fees.
